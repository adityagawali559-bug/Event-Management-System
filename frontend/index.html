How The System Works

Replacing manual forms, paper logs, and scattered WhatsApp groups with a unified digital workflow

1. Discover Events

Browse approved cleanups, blood drives, coding bootcamps, and cultural sessions with live seat availability.

2. Register / Volunteer

Instant 1-click registration or apply to be an event volunteer. Real-time capacity prevents overcrowding.

3. Stay Updated

Receive instant in-app alerts for announcements, schedule adjustments, and organizer notes.

4. Review & Impact

Submit verified ratings and reviews after event completion to help organizers measure civic impact.

Why CEMPS?

Transforming Manual Coordination Into Digital Efficiency

Zero Paper Waste & Instant Capacity Tracking

Automatic seat counters prevent duplicate signups and ensure event organizers stay within venue capacity limits.

Administrative Governance & Quality Control

Every community event submitted by an organizer undergoes Dean/Admin review before being published publicly.

Actionable Analytics for College Accreditation

Tracks student volunteer hours, community participation, and organizer feedback for college CEP evaluations.
